## Events

### The Wandering Merchant

> A merchant with a mule train of curiosities arrives at your capital. Beneath a silk cloth something glows faintly. "Everything has a price, friend. Some things have two."

- Buy the glowing relic (120 ): a random Rare or Epic boon.
- Buy his maps (40 ): reveal the surrounding land and a hidden cache.
- Send him on his way.

### Ruins in the Wilds

> Scouts report a buried city, older than any record. Its gates are sealed with strange sigils. Something might still guard it.

- Excavate the inner vaults (60%: a burst of science / 40%: awaken its guardians).
- Loot the outer halls and sell what you find.
- Leave the dead in peace.

### Sickness in the Streets

> A fever spreads through the crowded districts of your capital. The physicians ask for funds; the priests ask for prayer; the people ask for nothing but to live.

- Fund the physicians (80 ).
- Hold days of prayer (40 ).
- Let it run its course.

### A Sellsword Company

> A hard-bitten mercenary captain offers you his company's swords. "We fight for whoever pays. Today, that could be you."

- Hire them (100 ): 2 of your best melee units.
- Seize their pay-chest (60 , but they turn on you).
- Decline.

### A Comet Blazes

> A comet hangs over the land for nine nights. Everyone agrees it means something. Nobody agrees on what.

- Proclaim it an omen of glory: a 6-turn Golden Age.
- Study it: +40% of your current research.
- Declare it a sign from the heavens: Faith.

### Refugees at the Gates

> Thousands flee a war beyond your borders and gather outside your walls. They are hungry, tired, and many.

- Welcome them: capital +2 , but -1 Happiness in every city for 8 turns.
- Put them to work: a free Worker and a 10-turn production boom.
- Turn them away.

### The Stranger in Black

> He appears in your throne room without passing a single guard. "I can give you what you want most. The price is small. Just a little... misfortune."

- Accept the great pact: a random Legendary boon and 2 Curses.
- Accept a lesser pact: a random Epic boon and 1 Curse.
- Refuse.

### The Grand Tournament

> Champions from every land gather for a tournament of arms. Your own Champion is eager to compete. (Win chance: 40%)

- Enter your Champion (40% to win glory, XP and Culture).
- Host the games without competing (50 ).
- Ignore it.

### The Lost Library

> Beneath a collapsed temple your workers uncover shelves of sealed scrolls - the library of a forgotten dynasty.

- Copy the scrolls: +50% of your current research.
- Sell them to collectors: Gold.
- Display them publicly: Culture.

### Bandits on the Roads

> Bandits have seized a mountain pass and demand a toll from every caravan bound for your cities.

- Pay the toll (60 ).
- Root them out: 3 bandit units appear near one of your cities.
- Ignore them: -10% Gold in all cities for 10 turns.

### Gold in the Hills

> A shepherd stumbles into your court with a nugget the size of a fist. There is more where it came from.

- Stake a claim: a new Gold resource appears near one of your cities.
- Pan the streams right now: Gold.

### Earthquake!

> The ground heaves. Walls crack, towers fall, and dust hangs over your capital for days.

- Rebuild at once (70 ): +10% Production for 8 turns as crews keep working.
- Let the people rebuild: capital takes heavy damage and -10% Production for 8 turns.

### A Wild-Eyed Prophet

> A ragged prophet preaches in the market that the end is near - and that your reign is either its cause or its cure. Crowds hang on every word.

- Embrace his message: Faith, but unrest for 10 turns.
- Give him a stage: +25% Culture for 10 turns.
- Exile him: Culture.

### A Foreign Spy

> Your guards catch a foreigner copying the plans of your granaries. Under his cloak: letters in a rival's cipher.

- Interrogate him: +30% of current research.
- Ransom him back: Gold.
- Make an example of him: 10 turns of national pride (+1 Happiness per city).

### Volcanic Soil

> An old volcano has gone quiet. Its ash has turned the valleys below into the richest farmland anyone has seen.

- Fund new farming villages (100 ): gain the Fertile Lands boon.
- Quarry the obsidian: a production burst in every city.

### The Gambler's Den

> Your treasurer has found a den where the dice are said to be honest. He would like to try his luck - with the state's money.

- A modest bet (50 ): 50% to double it.
- Bet a quarter of the treasury: 40% to triple it.
- Walk away.

### The Legendary Smith

> A smith whose blades are spoken of in songs offers to work for you - for a single season.

- Arm your army (90 ): all military units +15 XP.
- Forge a blade for your Champion (60 ): +40 XP.
- Decline.

### A Bumper Harvest

> The granaries overflow. It is the best harvest in living memory.

- Hold a great feast: +1 Happiness per city for 10 turns.
- Sell the surplus: Gold.
- Store it: +10% Food in all cities for 15 turns.

### The Cursed Idol

> Explorers return with a golden idol, warm to the touch. Every one of them has had the same nightmare since they found it.

- Melt it down: lots of Gold, and a random Curse.
- Destroy it in a holy fire: Faith.
- Bury it where it was found.

### A Village in Peril

> A frontier village begs for help against a monster that takes their cattle - and lately, their children.

- Send your Champion: +30 XP and Culture, but it is wounded.
- Send hunters with a bounty (40 ).
- Ignore them: unrest for 8 turns.

### The Mysterious Cartographer

> A veiled woman with ink-stained fingers offers to draw you a map. "Of lands," she says, "or of fate. Choose."

- A map of fate: an extra draft.
- A map of chances: +2 Rerolls.
- A map of lands: reveal everything within 15 tiles of your capital.

### Deserters!

> Unpaid and homesick, a regiment threatens to desert. Their officers are waiting for your answer.

- Pay their back wages (60 ).
- Let them go: lose one random military unit.
- Hang the ringleaders: unrest for 10 turns, but the rest fight harder (+10 XP all).

### A Generation of Genius

> Something is in the water. Your academies overflow with prodigies, and one among them stands above the rest.

- Nurture the scientist: a free Great Scientist.
- Nurture the engineer: a free Great Engineer.
- Nurture the merchant: a free Great Merchant.

### Horde Scouts Sighted

> Riders in Horde colours have been seen watching your walls. An attack is coming - the question is when, and how ready you will be.

- Muster the militia: all cities +10 Defense for 10 turns, and the next raid is delayed 5 turns.
- Ambush the scouts: 2 weak Horde units appear (free XP).
- Ignore them: the next raid brings 2 extra units.

### The Great Debate

> Your court is divided over the future of the realm. Whichever faction you favour will shape the next opportunity you are offered. (Your next draft will be weighted heavily toward that theme.)

- The generals: War.
- The merchants: Commerce.
- The scholars: Knowledge.
- The farmers: Growth.

### Festival of Lights

> The guilds propose a festival of lanterns, music and theatre, the grandest your empire has ever seen. They only need a royal patron.

- Be their patron (50 ): +25% Culture and +1 Happiness per city for 10 turns.
- Decline.

### The Ancient Shrine

> Pilgrims lead you to a shrine older than your gods. It is said to wash away misfortune - or to bring it, to the unworthy.

- Pray at the shrine (60%: cleanse a Curse or gain Faith).
- Strip its gold: Gold, but -1 Faith per city for 15 turns.
- Leave an offering and go.

### The Lost Caravan

> A rich foreign caravan has lost its way in your deserts. Its guards are dying of thirst. Its cargo is worth a fortune.

- Rescue them: Gold reward and +10% Gold for 10 turns.
- Take everything: a fortune in Gold and the permanent Cursed Gold curse.

### An Envoy from the Horde

> A Horde envoy rides in under a white banner. "Pay tribute, and my masters will look elsewhere. For a while."

- Pay tribute (120 ): the next raid is delayed 12 turns.
- Send back his head: +1 Happiness per city for 10 turns, but the next raid brings 3 extra units.

### The Eccentric Inventor

> An inventor begs for a royal workshop. His last machine exploded. His one before that changed agriculture forever.

- Fund the workshop (55%: a free technology / 45%: a fire and a production slump).
- Give him a modest stipend: +10% Science for 10 turns.
- Show him the door.

## Ascension levels (cumulative)

1. **Restless Horde** - Horde raids come 20% more often.
2. **Swelling Ranks** - Horde raids bring +1 unit.
3. **Veteran Raiders** - Horde units are Veterans (+15% Combat Strength).
4. **Envious Rivals** - AI empires receive an extra Boon every era.
5. **Last Stand** - If your Champion dies, the run ends.
6. **Lean Times** - All cities -10% Gold and -10% Production.
7. **Marked** - Begin the run with a random Curse.
8. **Warlords Ascendant** - Era Warlords are Ascendant (+25%) and bring 2 more escorts.
9. **Scarcity** - Drafts offer one fewer card.
10. **The Long Night** - Horde raids begin 10 turns earlier and grow twice as fast.
